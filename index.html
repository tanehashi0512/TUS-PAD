TUS-PAD

Video Examples for Operational-State Anomaly Detection

TUS-PAD (Tokyo University of Science Physics Anomaly Dataset) consists of seven operational-state anomaly detection scenarios. This page presents representative video examples from the three newly collected scenarios: Metronome, Ski, and Gym.
